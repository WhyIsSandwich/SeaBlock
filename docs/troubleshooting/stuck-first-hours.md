# Stuck in the First Hours

Use this page when your run feels blocked early and you are not sure whether the issue is setup, progression, or build design.

## Read this if...

- You followed getting-started steps but still cannot progress.
- Everything feels fragile and you are unsure what to fix first.
- You need a quick branch to the right troubleshooting page.

## 2-minute triage

1. Does your world start look like expected Sea Block ocean/island conditions?
2. Are expected recipes present for your researched technologies?
3. Is your main blocker fuel, space, or a missing recipe/settings mismatch?

## Decision table

| Situation                            | Go here next                                                                                                           | Why                                     |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Start conditions look wrong          | [Map generation and ocean issues](/troubleshooting/map-generation-and-ocean-issues)                                    | Fixes invalid world assumptions first   |
| Recipes/settings look inconsistent   | [Missing recipe and mod settings](/troubleshooting/missing-recipe-and-mod-settings)                                    | Most common hidden blocker              |
| Pack/version confidence is low       | [Install and version mismatch](/troubleshooting/install-and-version-mismatch)                                          | Likely wrong version                    |
| Setup is fine but progression stalls | [First Steps](/getting-started/first-steps) and [Science Bottleneck Playbook](/guides/progression/science-bottlenecks) | Helps triage what production is missing |

## Stabilization priorities

- Stabilize fuel and power before expanding footprint.
- Keep one reliable ore path running before experimenting.
- Solve one blocker at a time and don't get overwhemed.
