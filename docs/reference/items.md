# Notable Items

This page calls out item categories that most strongly affect progression and factory design decisions in SeaBlock 2.0.

## Read this if...

- You are unsure which item lines deserve early automation.
- You need to understand why a specific intermediate keeps bottlenecking progression.
- You want category-level guidance without full spoiler chains.

## Notable item categories

### Fuel intermediates

Fuel items and their intermediates determine whether power transitions feel smooth or fragile.

### Circuit tiers

Circuit-tier items are recurring bottlenecks that often gate science and infrastructure upgrades.

### Catalyst and specialty intermediates

These items usually matter less in volume and more in progression gating. Confirm current 2.0 behavior before scaling.

### High-tier metallurgy outputs

Late materials usually become the limiting factor for advanced science and high-output builds.

## Decision table

| Situation | Item category to prioritize | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Constant power recovery work | Fuel intermediates | Improves base stability first |
| Science unlocks are slow | Circuit tiers | Most science chains consume them heavily |
| New chain is blocked by one rare input | Specialty intermediates | Usually a progression gate, not a volume issue |
| Late game underproduces advanced products | High-tier metallurgy outputs | Core late throughput limiter |

## 2.0 notes

- Metal catalyst details should be cross-checked with official release/changelog notes.
