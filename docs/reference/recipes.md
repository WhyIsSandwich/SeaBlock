# Notable Recipes

This page highlights high-impact recipe families to help players decide what to prioritize in SeaBlock 2.0.

## Read this if...

- You are unsure which recipe chains are worth optimizing first.
- You are transitioning from older guide assumptions.
- You need quick context before building a full production block.

## High-impact recipe families

### Startup resource conversion chains

These are your first stable loops for foundational materials and should be treated as temporary-but-reliable infrastructure.

### Algae and fuel conversion chains

These recipes define early-to-mid power stability and strongly influence when you can scale automation.

### Filtration and ore targeting chains

These determine how efficiently you can produce blocker metals for science progression.

### Smelting/metallurgy upgrade chains

These recipes usually decide when your base moves from "functional" to "scalable."

## Decision table

| Situation | Focus recipe family first | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early science stalls | Startup resource conversion | Restores progression basics |
| Power instability | Algae/fuel conversion | Removes bottleneck affecting all systems |
| One metal blocks all progress | Filtration/ore targeting | Fastest path to unblock science |
| Throughput cannot keep up | Smelting/metallurgy upgrades | Largest production leverage |

## 2.0 notes

- Stone-normalized recipe assumptions replace many old crushed-stone expectations.
- Validate old planner/chart recipes against current in-game 2.0 definitions.
