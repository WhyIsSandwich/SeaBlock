# Wiki Roadmap (Consumers and Maintainers)

This roadmap tracks wiki experience and editorial operations for readers, contributors, and maintainers.

## Document Control

- **Audience**: Consumers and Maintainers (Track A)
- **Owner area**: Docs maintainers
- **Target location**: `docs/reference`
- **Review cadence**: Monthly

## Now (0-1 Month)

1. **Improve reference discoverability**
   - Tighten cross-linking between troubleshooting, guides, and reference landing pages.
   - Ensure high-frequency terms and short answers are reachable in one click from `reference/index`.

2. **Harden contributor maintenance workflow**
   - Keep wanted-pages and screenshot backlog entries current and actionable.
   - Require page-level ownership and update notes for high-traffic docs.

3. **Raise publishing reliability for maintainers**
   - Keep maintainer-facing checks aligned with publishing workflows.
   - Ensure wiki updates follow a consistent contributor checklist.

## Next (1-3 Months)

1. **Close reference quality gaps**
   - Finish enhancement backlog items for FAQ, migration, planners, and compatibility pages.
   - Improve clarity and consistency in section structures across reference pages.

2. **Improve screenshot operations**
   - Convert high-priority screenshot stubs into captured assets with version annotations.
   - Archive obsolete images when UI or recipe chains change.

3. **Editorial governance cleanup**
   - Remove or merge duplicate contributor-facing planning content.
   - Keep one canonical location for each consumer/maintainer initiative.

## Later (3+ Months)

1. **Sustain content program**
   - Maintain a steady backlog of reference improvements tied to player demand signals.
   - Add periodic quality reviews for aging pages.

2. **Roadmap maintenance automation**
   - Periodically refresh roadmap status from tracked backlog pages and doc updates.
