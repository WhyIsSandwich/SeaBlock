## Gameplay

### Is Seablock mostly a peaceful run?

Most runs are logistics and process focused, with worms as the primary hostile pressure in relevant areas.

### Should I scale everything early?

No. Build temporary blocks for early milestones, then refactor as better processes unlock.

### Why are people using shorthand like "Algae 2" and "Ore Sorting 3"?

That shorthand tracks recipe/tech tier progression and is common across guides and discussions.

### Do old 1.1 guides still work in 2.0?

Partially. Concepts often still help, but recipe timings and progression assumptions can differ.

## Troubleshooting

### A recipe in a guide is missing. What first?

Confirm version alignment and startup setting assumptions before rebuilding your base around the missing step.

### My run feels impossible early on. Is that normal?

Early Seablock is intentionally constrained; focus on small stable loops, not perfect ratios.

### How do I tell install problems from progression problems?

If clean-profile behavior is wrong, start with install/version checks. If clean-profile is correct, diagnose recipe/progression assumptions next.
