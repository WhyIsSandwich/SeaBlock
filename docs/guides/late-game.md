# Late Game Guide

> The late game in SeaBlock is where your factory grows into a true megabase. You’ll produce advanced circuits, modules, and prepare for rockets and space science.

## Read this if...

- Mid-game systems are stable and scaling bottlenecks now dominate your playtime.
- You are pushing high-tier circuits, modules, and sustained science throughput.
- You want repeatable megabase architecture rather than ad-hoc expansion.

## Milestones

1. Fully automate advanced circuit tiers and supporting petrochem chains.
2. Maintain stable multi-science output through chemical, production, utility, and space tiers.
3. Deploy beacon/module-driven production where ROI is clear.
4. Build power with large reliability margin (fuel or nuclear path as appropriate).
5. Transition into continuous rocket and endgame progression loops.

## Build patterns

- Separate bulk intermediates from science assembly to simplify throughput tuning.
- Use train/block interfaces with explicit IO contracts between production districts.
- Standardize module/beacon templates for repeatable upgrades.
- Keep debug instrumentation for UPS hotspots and fluid starvation points.

> Late game is when your factory stops being a base and starts being an ecosystem with opinions.

## Common mistakes

- Chasing perfect ratios before solving transport and power stability.
- Upgrading too many blocks to beacon/module layouts at once.
- Ignoring UPS until late, then discovering architecture-level bottlenecks.
- Launching one successful rocket and immediately promising yourself "just one more small optimization."

## Megabase Planning

- **Tileable Blocks**: Design science production modules that can be repeated.
- **Trains & Warehouses**: Move bulk resources efficiently.
- **Beacons & Modules**: Maximize output and conserve machine count.

By the end of late game you should:

- Automate all science packs up to space science
- Run a gigawatt-scale power grid
- Launch rockets continuously
- Scale towards the spaceship and true endgame of SeaBlock

> Congratulations—you’ve turned an oceanic wasteland into an industrial empire!
