# Worm-Island Expansion Strategy

Clearing worm-guarded islands is an essential part of expansion in SeaBlock.

## Read this if...

- You need more land but nearby islands are worm-guarded.
- Combat pushes keep failing or feel too expensive.

## Expansion timing checklist

- Replacement turrets/ammo flow is automated, not hand-crafted.
- Landfill and repair supplies are buffered before the push.
- You are not carrying irreplaceable inventory during the push.

## Practical push pattern

1. Scout target island and choose shortest safe landfill bridge.
2. Stage turrets and ammo in a temporary forward supply point.
3. Advance in short segments; secure each segment before pushing farther.

Later-game options include drive-by grenade runs, sniper turrets, plasma turrets, artillery, and nuclear artillery.

## Common mistakes

- Expanding too far in one direction (worms get stronger the further from spawn)
- Treating worm clearing as one-off action instead of doing it in smaller chunks
