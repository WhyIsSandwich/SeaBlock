# Early Power

Early SeaBlock power is a transition problem, not a single build. You begin on wind and hand-crafted fuel, then move through algae and charcoal processing into more scalable options.

## Problem

If power stalls, every chain stalls: slag, ore, circuits, and landfill all back up at once. Most early failures come from:

- Building too little fuel processing.
- Underestimating startup fuel buffer.
- Upgrading power before ore and fluid throughput can support it.

## Milestones

1. **Bootstrap:** Place all starting wind turbines and keep emergency cellulose fiber in inventory.
2. **First automation:** Green Algae I feeds cellulose fiber and wood pellets.
3. **Stability:** Charcoal path replaces hand-crafted fuel for normal operation.
4. **Scale:** Green Algae II and cleaner loops reduce footprint and babysitting.
5. **Bridge to mid game:** Transition from algae-heavy setups to stronger fuel paths as chemistry unlocks.

## Build Patterns

- Prefer a **self-contained fuel block** with clear input/output boundaries.
- Keep a **reserve chest/tank policy** for critical fuel intermediates.
- Isolate starter power from high-draw experiments so one mistake does not brown out your full base.
- Expand in modules you can copy, not one massive monolith.

## Common Mistakes

- Running power and smelting from the same tiny fuel buffer.
- Treating byproducts as waste before you have a clear sink.
- Overbuilding power generation while ore and science remain underfed.
